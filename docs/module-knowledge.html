knowledge

A knowledge graph: facts like works_at(alice, acme), rules and constraints, and a reasoner that proves answers (✓ proven with the facts and rules it rests on, ✗ contradicted, ? unknown). The reasoner is Jade's Rust engine (assets/jade_kg.wasm, ~400 KB, fetched on first use). Files, plain text:

<root>/facts.jsonl   one fact per line: {"id","fact","status","by","source","ts"}; status confirmed | proposed | rejected
<root>/rules.dl      rules and constraints

Facts an agent adds are "proposed" until your app (or its user) confirms them; answers name unconfirmed facts they rest on.

On its own, without createKit:

import * as m from 'https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/lucky-edgelord/Jade-agent-kit@v3.1.0/dist/knowledge.js';
